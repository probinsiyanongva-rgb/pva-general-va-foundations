Email Management & Inbox Triage

Output: Inbox Triage Sheet

Step 1 of 8

Learn

Before you start

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Classify incoming messages according to action and priority.
  • Identify messages requiring client approval.
  • Track follow-ups.
  • Work within a client's inbox rules and authority.
Key idea

Inbox management is not "answer everything." It is helping the client determine what needs attention, what can be handled, what is waiting, and what can safely be filed.

Learn

Before managing a client inbox

Agree on these before you touch a live inbox:

  • Priorities
  • Approval rules
  • Response expectations
  • Forwarding rules
  • Deletion/archiving rules
  • High-priority senders
Learn

Simple triage categories

CategoryUse when…
ReplyYou can respond directly within your authority.
Client reviewThe client needs to decide or approve.
ActionAnother administrative action is required.
WaitingYou are waiting for another person or event.
ReferenceThe message should be retained but does not require action.
ArchiveThe conversation is complete and no longer needs to remain active.
Learn

Priority, and never inventing authority

Priority: Consider urgency, importance, dependency, and client-defined priorities. Unread does not automatically mean urgent.

Never invent authority: If a customer requests a refund, contract change, financial commitment, or other consequential action, check the client's policy and your authority before acting.

Learn

Follow-up tracking

Track who, what, when sent, follow-up date, and status. A follow-up that exists only in your memory is easy to lose.
