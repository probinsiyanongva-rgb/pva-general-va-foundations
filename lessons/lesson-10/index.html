Key idea

A task list is useful only if it helps you decide what to do next and prevents important work from disappearing.

Learn

Urgent vs. important, and dependencies

Urgent vs. important: Urgency concerns how soon something needs attention. Importance concerns the consequence of not doing it. A task can be urgent but low impact, or important but not immediately urgent.

Dependencies: A dependency means one task cannot proceed until another action, decision, file, or person is available.
