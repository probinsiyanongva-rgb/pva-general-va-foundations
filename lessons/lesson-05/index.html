Calendar & Appointment Management

Output: Client Calendar Plan

Step 1 of 9

Learn

Before you start

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Schedule meetings without creating avoidable conflicts.
  • Account for time zones, working hours, buffers, and preparation time.
  • Handle confirmations, rescheduling, and meeting details.
  • Follow client-specific scheduling rules.
Key idea

Calendar management is not placing events on a calendar. It is protecting the client's time and making sure commitments fit together.

Learn

Before scheduling

  • Know the client's working hours and preferred meeting windows.
  • Know which meetings require buffers or preparation time.
  • Know whether you have authority to accept, decline, or move meetings.
  • Know the client's relevant time zone(s).
  • Know how cancellations and rescheduling should be handled.
Learn

Time-zone discipline

Never assume that "3 PM" means the same thing for everyone. Confirm the intended time zone and make the zone visible when communicating with people in different regions.

Learn

Protect focus and transition time

A calendar that is technically conflict-free can still be operationally unrealistic. Consider travel, preparation, recovery, and focus blocks where the client requires them.

Learn

Meeting readiness

A useful event can include the correct title, participants, time zone, meeting link or location, agenda or preparation materials, and relevant notes.

Practice

Build the client's day

Client rules:
  • Working hours 9 AM–5 PM
  • Lunch 12–1 PM
  • No meetings during a 2-hour focus block (time not specified)
  • 15-minute buffer before a client presentation
Requests:
  • Team meeting 10–11 AM
  • Vendor call 11 AM–12 PM
  • Presentation 3–4 PM
  • Prospect call (30 minutes), "any time Tuesday afternoon"

Choose a start time for each item so the day follows every client rule. Then answer the question below.

One schedule that meets every rule:

4:00–5:00Open / wrap-up

Needs a client decision: the 2-hour focus block. With every request kept at its requested time, no 2-hour gap is left on this day, so ask the client whether to move a meeting or protect focus time on another day. Also confirm whether "Tuesday afternoon" has a preferred window for the prospect call.
