Learn

Before you start

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Identify appropriate administrative uses for AI.
  • Use AI to accelerate drafting, summarization, extraction, and organization.
  • Verify AI output before using it.
  • Protect confidential information.
  • Understand where human judgment remains necessary.
Key idea

AI can help produce work faster. The VA remains responsible for checking whether the output is accurate, appropriate, safe, and consistent with the client's instructions.

Learn

Appropriate uses

  • Drafting routine emails for review
  • Summarizing long notes or messages
  • Extracting action items
  • Turning unstructured notes into a first-draft checklist
  • Creating spreadsheet-formula suggestions
  • Reformatting information
  • Brainstorming categories or structures
  • Drafting an SOP from verified instructions
Learn

Verify before using

  • Check names, dates, numbers, links, and claims.
  • Check whether the output followed the client's instructions.
  • Check tone and context.
  • Check for invented information.
  • Check that sensitive information was handled appropriately.
Learn

Privacy rule

Do not paste confidential client or customer information into an AI service simply because it is convenient. Follow the client's policy and the approved tool configuration.

Practice

Verify the AI's work

"Hi! Can you draft a quick update for our vendor about the delayed shipment, remind me to call the accountant by Friday, and also please schedule a call with the new supplier sometime next week? Also, can you check if the invoice from last month (INV-2291) was already paid? Thanks!" — Dana, Coastal Pet Supplies

An AI tool drafted this action list from the email above. Check the boxes for items that are accurate and safe to use as-is — leave unchecked anything that's wrong, invented, or needs correction.
