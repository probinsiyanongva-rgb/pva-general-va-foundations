Internet Research & Information Management

Output: Client Research Brief

Step 1 of 8

Learn

Before you start

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Translate a client's question into a research task.
  • Find and evaluate useful sources.
  • Cross-check important information.
  • Separate facts from assumptions.
  • Present findings in a decision-ready format.
Key idea

Research is not simply searching Google. The output should help the client understand something, compare options, or make a next decision.

Learn

Research workflow

QUESTION→ SEARCH→ VERIFY→ ORGANIZE→ SUMMARIZE→ CITE
Learn

Source quality

  • Prefer primary sources when the question concerns a company's own product, policy, documentation, or official data.
  • Use reputable secondary sources for context and independent comparison.
  • Check dates when information can change.
  • Cross-check important claims.
  • Record the source for each meaningful factual claim.
Learn

Decision-ready research

Do not overwhelm the client with copied information. Organize findings around the decision criteria.
