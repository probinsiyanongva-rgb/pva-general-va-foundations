The engagement

Northstar Home Services

Northstar Home Services is a fictional five-person home-services business. The owner is spending too much time on routine administration and wants help keeping information, scheduling, and follow-ups organized.

The owner sends you a folder, an inbox simulation, a calendar, a customer spreadsheet, and several recurring instructions. Your job is to process the work in a sensible order and document your decisions.

Treat this like a real engagement

Understand the requests, clarify what's necessary, prioritize, execute, check, report, and close — the same eight-stage workflow from Lesson 2, applied across all 8 tasks below.

Task 2 of 8

Calendar

Client rules:
  • Working hours 8 AM–5 PM
  • No meetings on Wednesday mornings (the owner does site visits)
  • 30-minute prep buffer before any customer walk-through
  • The main supplier is based in a different time zone (UTC+9) — Northstar is UTC+8
Requests:
  • Customer walk-through — customer asked for "Wednesday at 9 AM"
  • Supplier call — supplier proposed "3 PM their time"
  • Team check-in — flexible, "sometime this week"
  • Insurance agent call — "Friday afternoon"
ItemPlacement & reasoning
Customer walk-throughThe requested Wednesday 9 AM conflicts with the no-meetings rule — propose Wednesday afternoon or Thursday morning instead, with a 30-minute buffer before it.
Supplier call3 PM their time (UTC+9) = 2 PM Northstar time (UTC+8) — schedule for 2 PM, noting the conversion in the invite.
Team check-inNo fixed constraint — slot in an open block, e.g. Monday 10 AM.
Insurance agent callFriday afternoon as requested — e.g. Friday 2 PM.

Needs owner confirmation: the walk-through's actual new time (since the customer's original ask conflicts with the Wednesday-morning rule), and confirming the supplier's time zone assumption before sending the invite.
