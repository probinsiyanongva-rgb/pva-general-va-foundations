The Support Ticket Workflow

Output: Ticket Action Plan

Step 1 of 7

Learn

Before you start

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Understand the lifecycle of a support case.
  • Separate the customer's problem from the underlying need.
  • Identify missing information.
  • Choose an appropriate next action.
  • Close a case only when the outcome is documented.
Key idea
RECEIVE→ UNDERSTAND→ INVESTIGATE→ DECIDE→ RESPOND→ DOCUMENT→ FOLLOW UP/CLOSE
Learn

Receive → Understand → Investigate

RECEIVE

Read the full case and context.

UNDERSTAND

Identify what happened, what the customer wants, and the desired outcome.

INVESTIGATE

Check approved systems, history, policy, and knowledge.

Learn

Decide → Respond

DECIDE

Resolve, request information, seek approval, or escalate.

RESPOND

Give a clear answer and realistic next step.

Learn

Document → Follow up/Close

DOCUMENT

Record important facts, actions, promises, and status.

FOLLOW UP/CLOSE

Follow up when required; close according to process.

Practice

What do you need to investigate?

"My package says delivered, but I don't have it."
A reasonable list: order number, delivery date/time, delivery address, carrier information (tracking, delivery photo/signature if available), any prior contacts about this order, and the client's delivered-but-not-received policy.
