Step 1 of 6

Learn

Before you start

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Adapt responses to email and live chat.
  • Keep responses concise without losing important information.
  • Maintain context across messages.
  • Use approved templates without sounding robotic.
  • Know when to escalate.
Key idea

Templates support consistency; they do not replace reading the customer's actual situation.

Learn

Email, and chat

Email:

  • Answer the actual question.
  • Separate multiple answers clearly.
  • Include the next step and timeframe when known.
  • Check links and attachments.

Chat:

  • Acknowledge quickly.
  • Ask focused questions.
  • Keep the customer oriented to what you are doing.
  • Avoid long blocks of text.
  • Summarize the outcome before ending.
