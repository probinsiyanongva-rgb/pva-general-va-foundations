Quality Assurance & Continuous Improvement

Output: QA Review

Step 1 of 6

Learn

Before you start

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Review responses before sending.
  • Identify accuracy, policy, tone, and completeness issues.
  • Recognize repeat problems that need process improvement.
  • Escalate serious or systemic issues.
Key idea

Quality support is more than a polite response: it is accurate, policy-aligned, customer-specific, and clear about the next step.

Learn

The five-question check

CheckQuestion
Correct?Are facts and policy interpretation accurate?
Complete?Did I address relevant parts of the request?
Consistent?Does the response match approved policies/templates?
Compliant?Did I stay within authority and protect data?
Clear?Does the customer know what happens next?
Learn

Common QA errors

  • Wrong customer/order number
  • Incorrect policy
  • Unsupported promise
  • Missing next step
  • Unnecessary jargon
  • Template mismatch
  • Poor internal note
  • Missing follow-up
  • Privacy mistake
  • Failure to escalate
