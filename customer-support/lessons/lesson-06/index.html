Before you start

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Collect relevant case information.
  • Use approved systems appropriately.
  • Separate evidence from assumptions.
  • Document findings clearly.
  • Know when investigation is sufficient.
Key idea

The fastest professional response is the fastest accurate path to the right outcome — not simply the fastest sentence.

Facts vs. assumptions

"Tracking says delivered" is a fact. "The customer received it" is an assumption. Support agents should distinguish what the system confirms from what they infer.

Conflicting information

Customer message (Aug 15): "I never got my order — please refund me." No prior contacts on this order. Client policy: refunds require confirming the delivery address matches the order, or a carrier investigation if addresses match.
