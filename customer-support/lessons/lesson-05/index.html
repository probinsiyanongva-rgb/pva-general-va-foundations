Learn

Before you start

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Use a knowledge base to find approved answers.
  • Distinguish policy from opinion.
  • Follow SOPs consistently.
  • Recognize policy gaps.
  • Know when approval is required.
Key idea

"I think this is probably okay" is not a substitute for an approved policy.

Learn

Knowledge-base workflow, and unclear policy

Knowledge-base workflow: Search using the customer's issue, product/service, error message, or policy term. Confirm the article is current and applies to the case.

When policy is unclear:

  • Do not invent a policy.
  • Do not use an outdated article as current.
  • Flag conflicting instructions.
  • Ask an authorized person for a decision.
  • Document the clarification.
Reference

This lesson's knowledge base

Five approved articles. You'll match tickets to them in the next step.

Shipping Delay
If a shipment is delayed beyond the promised date, apologize, share the latest tracking update, and offer to escalate to the carrier if delayed more than 5 business days.
Damaged Item
For damaged items reported within 14 days of delivery with photo evidence, offer a replacement or refund at the customer's choice.
Refund Eligibility
Refunds are available within 30 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. Digital and final-sale items are not eligible.
Password Reset
Direct customers to the "Forgot Password" link. If they don't receive the reset email within 10 minutes, verify the email on file and manually trigger a reset.
Escalation Rules
Escalate to a supervisor for: requests outside standard policy, threats of legal action, repeated unresolved contacts (3+), or safety concerns.
