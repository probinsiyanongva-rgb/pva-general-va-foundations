Learn

Before you start

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

  • Write useful internal notes.
  • Track status and ownership.
  • Record promises and follow-ups.
  • Prevent repeated work and lost cases.
  • Separate customer-facing and internal information.
Key idea

Good documentation lets another authorized teammate understand the case without making the customer repeat everything.

Learn

What a useful ticket note includes

FieldExample
IssueCustomer reports damaged item on order #1234
InvestigationPhoto received; order shipped 04/10; policy permits replacement
ActionReplacement requested
Customer updateCustomer informed of process
Next stepWarehouse confirmation pending
Follow-upCheck status tomorrow
Learn

Document facts, not judgments

Prefer: "Customer contacted support three times regarding the same order."
Avoid: "Customer is annoying."

Internal notes should remain professional and useful — a judgment tells the next reader how you felt, a fact tells them what actually happened.
