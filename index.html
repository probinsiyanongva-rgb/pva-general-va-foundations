A note on how the lessons are built
Not software tutorials

You won't find "how to create a Google Calendar event." You'll find how to schedule a meeting while respecting working hours, time zones, and prep time. The tool is the vehicle — the judgment is the lesson.

Fictional, not risky

Every inbox, calendar, and spreadsheet you'll touch is fictional and built into the lesson. You never connect a real account.

Reasoning, not just answers

When you choose an option, the lesson explains why it's right or wrong — not just a green check or red X.

Market info, not hype

No income promises, no "six-figure VA" talk. This training helps you understand the work and build real skill.
